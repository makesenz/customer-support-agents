# Order Changes & Cancellations

## Can I cancel my order?
- **Before dispatch:** yes. Contact support at +91 90425 12304 with your order ID. Prepaid orders are refunded in full to the original payment method within 3–7 business days. The ₹300 COD advance is **not** refunded.
- **After dispatch:** the order cannot be cancelled. You may return it unopened within 10 days of delivery (see `refund_policy.md`).

Orders placed before 3 PM IST on working days are processed the same day, so contact us as early as possible.

## Can I change my delivery address or phone number?
Yes, **before dispatch**. WhatsApp +91 90425 12304 with your order ID and the new address with pincode. After dispatch, we can only try to update it with the courier, and this is not guaranteed.

## Can I change the product or pack size in my order?
Yes, before dispatch. If the new pack costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, we refund the difference.

## Can I request discreet packaging after ordering?
Yes, before dispatch. Message support with your order ID.

## How do I know my order is confirmed?
- **Website orders:** you receive an order confirmation from www.maawellness.in.
- **WhatsApp orders:** our team confirms in the chat after receiving your details and payment or COD advance.

## I placed a duplicate order by mistake.
Contact support with both order IDs before dispatch. We will cancel the extra order and refund it if it was prepaid.
