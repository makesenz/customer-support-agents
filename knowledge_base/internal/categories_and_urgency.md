# Ticket Categories, Urgency & Sentiment

## Categories (choose exactly one)
| Category | Covers | Main KB file |
|---|---|---|
| Shipping | Delivery time, tracking, late orders, international, discreet packing | shipping_policy.md |
| Refund | Returns, refund status, "didn't work" | refund_policy.md |
| Damaged / Wrong Item | Broken, leaking, wrong or missing items, broken seal | damaged_or_wrong_item.md |
| Billing / Payment | UPI, COD advance, double charge, payment not confirmed | payment_and_cod.md |
| Order Change | Cancel, change address, change pack | order_management.md |
| Account | Login, password, registration, data deletion | account_and_login.md |
| Product Info | Ingredients, usage, which pack, prices | product_catalog.md, pricing_and_offers.md |
| Health / Safety | Side effects, medical conditions, other medicines | usage_and_safety_faq.md |
| Other | Anything else (bulk, distributor, fake sites, media) | company_overview.md |

## Urgency
| Level | When |
|---|---|
| **High** | Side effect or health reaction; payment taken but no order, or double charge; legal threat; order 7+ working days late; fraud or fake seller |
| **Medium** | Damaged or wrong item; refund request; order late but within 7 working days; cancellation before dispatch |
| **Low** | General questions: pricing, usage, login help, shipping time |

## Sentiment
`Positive` · `Neutral` · `Frustrated` · `Angry` · `Very Angry`

"Very Angry" = abusive language, all caps, threats (bank, lawyer, social media, police), or repeated complaints.
