# Maa Wellness – Company Overview & Contact

## Who are we?
Maa Wellness is an Ayurvedic wellness brand for men and women. Our products are made from 25+ natural herbs and seeds in a GMP-certified facility. The brand was founded in August 2020 in Tamil Nadu by Mr. A.K. Sheik Meeran, a medicine specialist with 14+ years of experience.

- Customers served: 2.5 lakh+
- Exporting to: 50+ countries

## What products do you sell?
1. **Mr. Perfect Men Wellness**: Ayurvedic Rasayana powder for men
2. **Ms. Perfect Women Wellness**: Ayurvedic powder for women
3. **Herbal Massage Oil**: external-use oil for men

See `product_catalog.md` for details and `pricing_and_offers.md` for prices.

## Do you do pulse diagnosis (நாடி பரிசோதனை)?
No. Our centre and online consultations offer **lifestyle and wellness counselling only**: diet guidance, stress management and sexual-wellness education. Physical pulse diagnosis is **not** performed.

## Do you follow up after I buy?
Yes. Customers receive wellness tips and check-ins during their course. For example, we follow up when you finish each tin (about every 12–13 days).

## Which websites are official? How do I avoid fake sellers?
- The only official website is **www.maawellness.in**.
- Any other website using our product names or images is **not official**. Do not order or pay there.
- Reselling our products on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp or other portals is not authorised. We cannot guarantee products bought from unofficial sellers.
- Only pay to the official payment details listed in `payment_and_cod.md`.
