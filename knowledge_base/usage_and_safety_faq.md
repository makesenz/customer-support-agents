# Usage & Safety FAQ

## Are the products safe? Do they have side effects?
Our products are made from natural Ayurvedic herbs and seeds in a GMP-certified facility. Ms. Perfect is non-hormonal and steroid-free. Natural products can still affect each person differently. If you notice any discomfort, **stop using the product and consult a doctor**.

## Can I take it with my other medicines or supplements?
Please **consult your doctor** before taking our products with other medicines or supplements. This is especially important if you have diabetes, high or low blood pressure, heart, kidney or liver conditions, or take regular medication. Our wellness counsellors can share general guidance, but they do not replace your doctor.

## Can I take it during pregnancy or while breastfeeding?
Please consult your doctor before using any supplement during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

## Is it a medicine? Will it cure my problem?
Our products are Ayurvedic wellness supplements that **support** the body's natural health. They are not a replacement for medical treatment, and results vary from person to person. For a diagnosed medical condition, please continue to follow your doctor's advice.

## Is there an age limit?
The products are meant for adults. For older adults or people with health conditions, please consult a doctor first.

## When is the best time to take Mr. Perfect?
Twice daily **after food** (morning and night): 2 level teaspoons in warm milk or warm water.

## When is the best time to take Ms. Perfect?
Once daily **after dinner**: 1–2 teaspoons in warm water or warm milk.

## I missed a dose. What should I do?
Just continue with your next regular dose. Do not take a double dose.

## Can I take more than the recommended dose for faster results?
No. Please follow the recommended dose. Taking more does not give faster results, and the tin will finish sooner.

## How should I store the products?
Keep the tin tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Always use a dry spoon so moisture does not get into the powder.

## Can women take Mr. Perfect, or men take Ms. Perfect?
No. Mr. Perfect is formulated for men, and Ms. Perfect is formulated for women.

## Can the massage oil be swallowed?
No. The Herbal Massage Oil is **for external use only**. Avoid the urethral tip, eyes and broken skin. If irritation occurs, wash it off and stop using it.
