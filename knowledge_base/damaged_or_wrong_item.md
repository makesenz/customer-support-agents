# Damaged, Leaking, Missing or Wrong Items

## My product arrived damaged, broken or leaking. What do I do?
1. Report it **within 48 hours of delivery**.
2. WhatsApp **+91 90425 12304** or email **maanaturalsmrperfect@gmail.com** with:
   - Your order ID
   - Clear **photos** of the damaged product and the outer box
   - An **unboxing video**, if you have one (this speeds up approval)
3. After verification, we send a **free replacement**. If a replacement is not possible, we give a refund to your original payment method.

You do not need to pay for the replacement shipping.

## The tamper-evident seal was broken when I received it.
Do not use the product. Send photos within 48 hours and we will replace it.

## I received the wrong product or the wrong quantity.
Report it within 48 hours with your order ID and a photo of what you received. We will send the correct product. Our team will tell you if the wrong item needs to be returned.

## The free massage oil was missing from my combo.
Combos include free oils (4+1, 8+2, 12+3). If the oil is missing, report it within 48 hours with a photo of the package contents. We will send the missing item.

## The powder looks, smells or tastes different from before.
Natural herbal powders can vary slightly in colour and smell between batches. If you believe the product is spoiled (damp, lumpy with mould, or a bad smell), stop using it and send photos plus the batch details on the tin. Our quality team will check it.

## How long does a replacement take?
Replacements are dispatched within **1–2 working days** after approval. Normal delivery times apply (see `shipping_policy.md`).
