# Product Catalog

## Mr. Perfect Men Wellness (Rasayana Powder)

### What is it for?
A natural Ayurvedic supplement designed to support men's stamina, energy, vitality, timing and reproductive wellness. It is used by men facing fatigue, low libido, performance concerns and low semen quality.

### What are the ingredients?
28+ classical Ayurvedic herbs and seeds, including Purified Shilajit, Ashwagandha, Safed Musli, Gokshura, Kaunch Beej (Velvet Bean / Mucuna pruriens), Drumstick (Moringa) seeds, Flax seeds, Pumpkin seeds, Nutmeg and natural bio-enhancers.

### How do I take it?
- **2 level teaspoons**, mixed in **warm milk or warm water**
- **Twice daily after food** (morning and night)
- Consistency is important. Take it every day.

### How long does one tin last?
One tin lasts **12 to 13 days** at the recommended dose. One tin is a **starter / trial pack**. A full course needs a minimum of **48 to 96 days (4 to 8 tins)**.

Classical female tonics including Shatavari, Lodhra, Ashoka bark, Amla, Tree Turmeric, Dill seed, Baheda, Flax seed, and nourishing dry fruits (cashew, almond, pistachio, dry dates).

### How do I take it?

- Non-hormonal and steroid-free.
