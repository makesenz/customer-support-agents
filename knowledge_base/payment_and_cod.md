# Payments & Cash on Delivery

## What payment methods do you accept?
1. **Online payment on the website** (www.maawellness.in), processed through a secure payment gateway
2. **UPI**: GPay, PhonePe, Paytm
3. **Direct bank transfer**: usually for high-value orders
4. **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: across India, with a ₹300 advance

## How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work?
- COD is available across India.
- A **₹300 advance by UPI** (maawellness@upi) is **mandatory** for every COD order. Your order is dispatched only after this advance is received. It prevents parcels being refused and returned (RTO).
- The ₹300 is an **extra charge on top of the product price**. It is **not** deducted from the product price.
- The ₹300 advance is **non-refundable**.
- You pay the **full product price** in cash to the courier at delivery.
- **100% prepaid orders save this ₹300** and get **priority dispatch**.

| Example (Mr. Perfect Single Pack, ₹2,900) | Prepaid | COD |
|---|---|---|
| Pay now | ₹2,900 | ₹300 advance by UPI |
| Pay at delivery | – | ₹2,900 cash |
| **Total** | **₹2,900** | **₹3,200** |

## I paid but my order is not confirmed. What should I do?
Send your payment screenshot, name and phone number on WhatsApp (+91 90425 12304). Our team will verify it and confirm your order.

## I was charged twice / extra money was deducted. What happens?
Share the transaction screenshots and your order ID with support. Our accounts team checks duplicate or failed payments. Any confirmed extra amount is refunded to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to post a refund. Payment disputes are always handled by a human support agent.

## Is my card information safe?
Yes. Online payments are processed by a secure payment gateway with SSL encryption. Card details are not stored on our servers.

## Do you send GST invoices?
Contact support with your order ID to request an invoice.
