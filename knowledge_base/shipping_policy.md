# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## When will my order be dispatched?
- Orders placed **before 3 PM IST on working days** (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays) are processed the **same day**.
- Please allow **1–2 working days** for your order to be packed and handed to the courier.
- **Prepaid orders get priority dispatch.** COD orders are dispatched after the ₹300 advance is received.

## How long does delivery take in India?
Delivery within India takes about **4 working days after dispatch**. Weekends and public holidays can add extra days.

**Total time from order to delivery is usually 5–6 working days.**

## Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship worldwide. International orders usually arrive within **7–15 working days after dispatch**. Customs checks, weekends and holidays can cause delays.

## Who pays customs duty for international orders?
The customer pays any customs duties, import taxes or extra charges in their country. Please check with your local customs office.

## How do I track my order?
Call or WhatsApp our support desk at **+91 90425 12304** with your order ID or registered phone number. We will share your courier tracking details.

## My order is late. What should I do?
1. Check whether **4 working days after dispatch** have passed (plus any weekend or holiday).
2. If it is late, contact support at +91 90425 12304 or maanaturalsmrperfect@gmail.com with your order ID.
3. We will check with the courier and give you an update.

If the order has not arrived **7 working days after dispatch** within India, it is treated as delayed and handled by a human support agent.

## Is the packaging discreet? Will anyone know what's inside?
- **Standard packaging:** sealed with our official tamper-evident seal.
- **Discreet packaging (on request):** 100% plain, unbranded packaging with **no brand name or labels** on the outside. Just ask for discreet packaging when you order (in WhatsApp or the order notes).

## The courier tried to deliver but I wasn't home.
Contact support quickly with your order ID. We will ask the courier to try again. Refused COD parcels are returned to us and the ₹300 advance is not refunded.
