# Website Account & Login

## Do I need an account to order?
No. You can order on WhatsApp (+91 90425 12304) without an account. An account on www.maawellness.in lets you view your orders, save your address and use a wishlist.

## How do I create an account?
1. Go to **https://maawellness.in/my-account/**
2. Fill in the **Register** form with your email address and password.
3. Click **Register**.

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
1. Go to **https://maawellness.in/my-account/lost-password/**
2. Enter your **username or registered email address**.
3. Click **Reset password**.
4. Open the email from Maa Wellness and click the **reset link**.
5. Set a new password and log in.

If you don't see the email within 10 minutes, check your **Spam / Promotions** folder. The reset link works only once. Request a new one if it has expired.

## I didn't receive the password reset email.
- Check the Spam and Promotions folders.
- Make sure you entered the same email you used to register.
- If it still doesn't arrive, email **maanaturalsmrperfect@gmail.com** from your registered email address and we will help.

## How do I see my past orders?
Log in at https://maawellness.in/my-account/ and open **Orders**.

## How do I change my name, email or address?
Log in and open **Account details** or **Addresses**. To update details you gave us on WhatsApp, message +91 90425 12304.

## How do I delete my account or my personal data?
Email **maanaturalsmrperfect@gmail.com** from your registered email. We do not sell or trade your personal information. See our privacy policy at https://maawellness.in/privacy-policy/.

## Do you keep my health concerns private?
Yes. Your personal information is only accessible to a limited number of authorised staff, who are required to keep it confidential.
