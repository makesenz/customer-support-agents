# Refund & Returns Policy

## How many days do I have to return a product?
You can request a return or refund **within 10 days of receiving your order**. Requests made after 10 days are not eligible.

## Which products can be returned?
To be eligible, the product must be:
- **Unused and unopened** (seal not broken)
- In the **same condition** as you received it
- In its **original packaging**
- With a **receipt or proof of purchase** (order ID, invoice or payment screenshot)

**Opened or used tins and oil bottles cannot be returned**, because these are consumable health products. If your product arrived damaged, wrong or leaking, see `damaged_or_wrong_item.md` instead.

## How do I request a return?
1. Email **maanaturalsmrperfect@gmail.com** or WhatsApp **+91 90425 12304** within 10 days of delivery.
2. Share your order ID, the reason for the return, and photos of the sealed product.
3. Our team will share the return instructions.

## How does the refund work?
1. When we receive your return, we inspect it and **email you** to confirm we received it.
2. We tell you whether the refund is **approved or rejected**.
3. Approved refunds are processed within **3–7 business days**.
4. The money is credited to your **original payment method** (UPI, card or bank account).

## Is the ₹300 COD advance refunded?
No. The ₹300 COD advance is **non-refundable**.

## I haven't received my refund yet.
1. Check your bank account and card statement again.
2. Contact your card company or bank. Refund posting can take **5–10 business days**.
3. If it is still missing, email **maanaturalsmrperfect@gmail.com** with your order ID.

## The product didn't work for me. Can I get a refund?
Results vary from person to person, and herbal supplements need regular use over several weeks. Opened products cannot be returned. Please contact our wellness team at +91 90425 12304. They can review how you are taking the product and guide you. A support agent will review special refund requests.
